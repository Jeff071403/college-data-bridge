1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PURPOSE
================================================================================
The MOU Lifecycle & Document Management System is an enterprise-grade platform 
specifically designed for universities and colleges to manage Memorandums of 
Understanding (MOUs), industrial partnerships, student internship agreements, 
and joint research initiatives.

Key Problem Solved:
- Replaces unorganized local drive folder storage with a centralized, searchable repository.
- Eliminates manual date tracking through automated expiry date calculations based on 
  the actual Signed Date (not upload date).
- Provides proactive warning alerts (30, 15, 7, and 1 day before expiration).
- Enables one-click renewal to seamlessly roll over expiring agreements without data loss.

3. USER ROLES & ACCESS CONTROL
================================================================================
1. Super Admin:
   - Full system privileges bypass checks.
   - Can manage all folders, documents, user accounts, and system settings.
   - Access to full security audit logs and custom permission overrides.

2. Lawyer / MOU Administrator (Admin Role):
   - Uploads original draft MOUs and assigns them to departments.
   - Configures dynamic MOU Templates and custom fields.
   - Reviews, approves, or rejects signed MOUs submitted by departments.
   - Executes One-Click Renewals.

3. Department User / Coordinator:
   - Accesses MOUs shared with their assigned department (Engineering, Medical, Commerce, etc.).
   - Uploads executed Signed MOU documents.
   - Sets actual Signed Date and fills summary/beneficiaries forms.
   - Previews calculated expiry dates.

4. View Only User:
   - Can search, view, and read assigned MOUs.
   - Cannot create, edit, approve, or delete records.

4. MOU LIFECYCLE & STATUS WORKFLOW
================================================================================
Every MOU progresses through a defined multi-step lifecycle:

  [1. Draft] ➔ [2. Shared] ➔ [3. Signed] ➔ [4. Pending Verification] ➔ [5. Active] ➔ [6. Expiring Soon] ➔ [7. Expired / Renewed]

Stage Details:
- Draft: MOU draft created by Administrator. Private to creator.
- Shared: MOU distributed to assigned department coordinators for review.
- Signed: Executed agreement document uploaded by department with Signed Date.
- Pending Verification: Agreement submitted for legal review and approval.
- Active: MOU approved by Administrator. Expiry date monitored automatically.
- Expiring Soon: Automatically triggered when Expiry Date is within 30 days.
- Expired / Renewed: Passed end date (Expired) or renewed into a new version (Renewed).

5. DYNAMIC MOU TEMPLATES & FIELD CUSTOMIZATION
================================================================================
The system supports customizable MOU Templates stored as JSON schemas. Administrators 
can create new templates on the `/templates` page without changing code.

Default Seeding Templates:
- Internship: Duration, Eligible Students, Monthly Stipend
- Placement: Eligible Departments, Expected CTC Package, Selection Process
- Research: Funding Amount, Research Domain, Principal Investigator
- Industry Collaboration: Co-branded Lab Setup, Industry Mentor

Template Notes:
Each template contains a "Template Notes" field where administrators explain the 
purpose of the template and how non-technical coordinators should complete the fields.

6. SIGNED DOCUMENT UPLOAD & SMART EXPIRY DATE CALCULATOR
================================================================================
Formula:
  Calculated Expiry Date = Signed Date + Duration (Months)

How It Works:
1. When uploading a signed MOU document, the user selects the actual `Signed Date` 
   using a date picker (defaults to system current date, but fully editable).
2. The duration (e.g. 12 Months) is read automatically from the agreement template.
3. The system computes the exact Expiry Date in real-time and displays a live preview:
   Example: Signed Date = 15 Aug 2026, Duration = 6 Months ➔ Expiry Date = 15 Feb 2027.
4. Expiry is ALWAYS computed from the Signed Date, NOT the upload timestamp.

7. APPROVAL, VERIFICATION & REJECTION WORKFLOW
================================================================================
1. Department submits signed MOU ➔ Status becomes `Pending Verification`.
2. Administrator receives an in-app notification and email alert.
3. Administrator reviews document details on `/mou/:id` page and clicks:
   - [Approve MOU]: Status transitions to `Active`. System records approval in audit log.
   - [Reject / Request Changes]: Administrator enters remarks. Status returns to `Draft`.

================================================================================
8. AUTOMATIC DAILY EXPIRY REMINDERS & NOTIFICATIONS
================================================================================
The background scheduler inspects all Active MOUs daily and fires notifications:
- 30 Days Before Expiry: Green warning alert sent to Admin & Coordinator.
- 15 Days Before Expiry: Amber warning alert.
- 7 Days Before Expiry: Orange urgent warning alert.
- 1 Day Before Expiry: Red critical warning alert.
- Expiry Day: Final expiration notification; status auto-updates to `Expired`.

Status Pill Colors & Badges:
- Green Pill + Check Icon: Active (> 30 Days remaining)
- Yellow/Orange Pill + Warning Icon: Expiring Soon (≤ 30 Days)
- Red Pill + Cancel Icon: Expired / Rejected
- Slate Pill + Edit Icon: Draft

12. OPERATIONAL STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
================================================================================
How to Create a New MOU:
1. Navigate to "MOU Repository" (`/mou-repository`) and click "+ Create New MOU".
2. Fill Step 1 (Title, Partner Org, Target Department, Template).
3. Fill Step 2 (Dynamic Template Fields & Summary).
4. Fill Step 3 (Duration, Coordinator Email, Partner Email).
5. Step 4 Review & Submit. MOU is saved and shared.

How to Submit a Signed MOU:
1. Navigate to `/mou-repository` or `/shared`.
2. Locate agreement in Draft status and click "Upload Signed".
3. Select Signed Date. Verify the computed Expiry Date in the live preview box.
4. Click "Submit & Verify".

How to Execute One-Click Renewal:
1. Open any Active or Expired MOU on `/mou/:id` page.
2. Click "One-Click Renewal".
3. System automatically clones previous data into a new renewal draft while keeping 
   historical version links intact.
